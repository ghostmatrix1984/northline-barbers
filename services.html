04 — Good to know

A few things
worth asking.

A few practical things before you come in.

How long does a haircut take?

Allow about 30–45 minutes for most cuts. Some services take a bit longer.

Do I need to know exactly what haircut I want?

No. A photo helps, but we can talk it through when you get here.

Can I book a haircut and beard trim together?

Yes. Book Cut & Beard so there’s time for both.

Do you take walk-ins?

Booking ahead is best. If we’ve got a gap on the day, we’ll fit you in.

What if I’m running late?

Give us a ring as soon as you can. If there isn’t enough time left for your cut, we may need to rebook you.
